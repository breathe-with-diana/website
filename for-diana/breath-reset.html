What to record

Around ten minutes, guiding one gentle practice, once per language you want live. This is a suggestion, not a script. If your instinct says something different, follow it.

0 to 1
Arriving. She is lying down, eyes closing. Nothing asked of her yet.
1 to 3
Noticing. The body as it already is. No fixing, no effort, no getting it right.
3 to 8
The breath. The gentle practice, the everyday one from your first door, not the deep work.
8 to 10
Coming back. Slowly. And a sentence about what she might feel afterwards, so she is not alone with it.

It should feel whole, not like a sample

The page promises her "a small taste of this work". That works best if the ten minutes are complete in themselves, something she could come back to on a hard evening, rather than a piece of something bigger that stops early.

How to record it

Your phone is genuinely enough. Nothing needs to be bought.

  • Voice Memos on your phone, held about a hand's width from your mouth, in the quietest room you have.
  • The one thing that ruins a recording is steady hum. Air conditioning, a fan, a fridge, traffic. It cannot be taken out afterwards, and it is the reason most home recordings have to be done again. Turn things off, and listen to the room for ten seconds before you start.
  • One continuous take beats a tidy one. Pauses, a breath, a small stumble, they all belong. Please do not stop and start again to fix a word, that is what makes a recording sound like an advert.
  • No music needed. If it wants music later, it can go underneath without you re-recording.
  • Send it exactly as your phone made it. No renaming, no converting, no trimming. Drop it into the folder Free Breath-Reset Audio inside 01 Brand & Assets on your Drive, or just send it to me. Only tell me which language it is, that is all I need.

Two things to hold on to

It is always your voice. Never a generated one, never someone else reading your words. This is the first time most people will hear you, and that has to be real.

Nothing is permanent. The recording sits on a page rather than inside the email, so if you listen back in a month and want to do it again, we swap the file and everyone gets the new one. Nothing breaks, nothing needs re-sending. So the first take does not have to be the perfect one.

This is not the voice note for your website

You have two recordings on your list and they are different things. The voice note is a few seconds of you greeting people on your new homepage. This breath reset is the ten minute practice above. If you only have energy for one, do the voice note first, it is much shorter and it is holding up your page.
